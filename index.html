SocialWox

Since day one

A business should look as good online as it does in real life.

SocialWox designs and builds websites for businesses that want to be taken seriously the moment someone lands on their page. We're not a template shop, and we don't specialise in one type of client — restaurants, retailers, studios, startups and long-running companies all come to us for the same reason: a site built with intent, not assembled from a kit.

Every project starts from the business itself — what it sells, who it serves, how it wants to be seen — and is built into a structure of typography, imagery and motion that carries that identity through every screen.

Our approach

The same four stages, on every project, regardless of industry.

01

Observe

We study the business, its customers and the field it competes in before a single pixel is placed.

02

Define

We set the visual and digital direction — typography, structure, tone — and agree it before building.

03

Build

We design and develop in parallel, so what you review is close to what will actually ship.

04

Refine

We tune performance, motion and detail until the site holds up as well under scrutiny as at first glance.
